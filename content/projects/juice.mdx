## Why This Works

By treating creative energy with the same formalism as electromagnetic fields or thermodynamics, the tracker:

1. **Provides precise measurement** — You&apos;re not just &ldquo;feeling good,&rdquo; you&apos;re measuring a 1.25 GQ with +0.10 Φf momentum.
2. **Encourages daily tracking** — The scientific units demand precision and consistency.
3. **Reveals actual patterns** — Tracking GQ vs Anxiety over time shows real correlations and trends.

## Core Metrics

The system tracks fundamental psychophysical quantities:

- **Daily Juice & Anxiety levels** — Calibrated 0-10 scales logged each day
- **Trend analysis** — 7-day moving averages to smooth out measurement noise
- **Pattern recognition** — Heatmaps reveal which days consistently drain or boost creative capacity
- **Long-term comparison** — Historical data shows whether baseline Juice levels are rising or falling
