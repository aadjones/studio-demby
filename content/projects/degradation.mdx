<ProjectOverview header={null}>
This began with a curiosity about visualizing latent space.

Images, living inside that space, being warped by slowly degrading text prompts. The instructions themselves and the algorithmic process began to introduce more and more noise.

*Degradation* is what happens when we reverse the diffusion process of text-to-image generation. The image is born of noise, and returns to noise.

The result: a looping entropy. Birds that flock into a melting streak of abstraction. A piano that smears into a grid of noise. A cathedral made of feathers, or a feather made of cathedrals.
</ProjectOverview>
