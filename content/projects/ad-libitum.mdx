<ProjectOverview header={null}>

The *Rara Avis* series by <a href="https://www.staciebirkygreene.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Stacie Birky Greene</a> reminds us of the fraught connection between humans and the natural world. The subjects are endangered and extinct birds, all teeming with personality. In my own series *Ad Libitum*, inspired by four of Stacie&apos;s pieces, I improvised short pieces evoking my mixture of feelings about the artwork, the birds themselves, and the weight of endangerment and extinction. Each improvisation starts from a simple notated kernel, viewable on the back of each card.

</ProjectOverview>
