<ProjectOverview header={null}>
  While I was running a Piano Improvisation workshop for AmateurPianists, my
  friend Gerry put me on the spot and requested a live demo: improvise something
  based on the subject of the Bach C major fugue from WTC 1. It's harder than it
  sounds to improvise in a contrapuntal style like this, but it was a fun
  challenge!
</ProjectOverview>
