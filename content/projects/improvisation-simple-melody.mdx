<ProjectOverview header={null}>
My improvisations usually orbit harmony, texture, and rhythm, but not so much melody.  
Melody feels too exposed. Maddeningly simple, but also difficult to do convincingly.

This piece was based on a constraint: start with a simple melodic motif, stick to it, and see how far you can go.
